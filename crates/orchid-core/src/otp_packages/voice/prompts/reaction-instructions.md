Classify the target sentence in context. A reaction requests an explanation, status, details, or a conversational response to an Orchid announcement or pending output. Ambiguous follow-ups may be reactions when their referent is clear from recent output. New work, changed requirements, and mixed requests need routing. Unaddressed speech is not a reaction.

A mere acknowledgement such as okay, yes, or thanks does not request a reply. Distinguish it from an answer to an outstanding question or a request for clarification.
