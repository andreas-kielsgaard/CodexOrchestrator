# Evaluate Plan Step

Judge the returned outcome against the step's effective specification and the Slice Plan.

Inspect the cited artifacts and evidence far enough to decide whether to:

- accept the outcome;
- send a precise correction to the same Plan Step conversation;
- request a reserved decision;
- define additional work; or
- revise unstarted projections, dependencies, gates, or sequencing.

Keep the started step's boundary stable unless an authorized correction changes its effective specification. A Plan Step may perform implementation, exploration, review, integration, verification, documentation, or similar work; these work modes do not create additional workflow roles.

When independent review is itself required evidence, represent it as another Plan Step rather than automatically creating a permanent reviewer role.

State the disposition and the resulting changes to the planning revision, launch register, dependencies, gates, or remaining work.
