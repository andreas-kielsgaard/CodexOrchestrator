# Ad-hoc Initiative plan format

Status: revised. Product code and product-owned role skills unchanged.

## Observation

Task `019fc08b-1f95-7981-be15-2dd600ff72f7` used the former `orchestration-plan-builder` for an explicitly ad-hoc plan. Its output adopted product-shaped state and startup concepts that were not available to the task.

The skill accurately produced what its mixed contract requested.

## Theory

The builder combined strategic planning for a Codex-run Initiative with a prospective product representation. Reusing Epic, Sprint, and Work Unit language also made the two catalogues look interchangeable.

## Revision

`initiative-planner` now produces a self-contained Markdown plan grounded in current evidence. It forecasts provisional Work Cycles while leaving current-state planning and Delivery Task design to the Work Cycle Coordinator.

Product capabilities may be planned as outcomes, but become planning infrastructure only when a verified interface is explicitly supplied. Startup remains a separately authorized action through `initiative-instantiator` and `start-initiative-tasks`.

## Evaluation

The smaller contract preserves objective, scope, problem relationships, provisional movements, risks, decisions, and completion evidence without imposing unavailable product formats. The distinct vocabulary reduces accidental skill crossover while retaining a clear planning altitude.
