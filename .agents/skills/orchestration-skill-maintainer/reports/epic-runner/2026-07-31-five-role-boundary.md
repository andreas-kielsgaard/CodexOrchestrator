# Epic Runner Five-Role Boundary

## Observation and theory

The earlier skill selected the next Sprint but said little about Epic organization or cross-Sprint integration. That made the role look like a thin child-launch step rather than the owner of Epic movement.

## Revision

The Epic Runner now reconciles Sprint outcomes, integration state, deferred boundaries, and the remaining trajectory. It selects concern-level Sprint movement and works only through the immediate Sprint Runner child. A ready-only harness stops at the truthful ready state.

## Evaluation

This keeps the role Epic-scoped and downward-focused without assigning it Sprint planning or implementation. The product currently supplies only the Epic Runner harness; later child actions remain conditional on application exposure.

## Validation

`quick_validate.py` passed. A fresh Epic Runner kept an unintegrated shared contract at Epic level, made it an explicit next-Sprint concern, created no Work Units, and stopped at the harness's truthful ready-only boundary.
