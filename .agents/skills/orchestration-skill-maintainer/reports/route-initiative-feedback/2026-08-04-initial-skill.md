# Route Initiative Feedback

## Observation and theory

Review Coach and Prepare Human Loop produce feedback outside an active Initiative that may affect its direction or owned work. The initial ingress contract required the Initiative owner to return every disposition to the originating interface. Later wording removed that loop, but did not require the ingress reader to establish that a multi-turn review had reached a coherent routing boundary. The HIL session consequently sent an open review slice after an intermediate user response.

## Revision concept

Keep the Overall Plan owner as the external ingress. Before sending, establish that the originating review or human contribution is closed, or that the user explicitly requested early dispatch. Preserve intermediate observations with the originating session. Identify affected internal work as context and leave Initiative-owned routing to the owner. Request a result only when it changes currently authorized human-facing work; otherwise complete the operation at delivery.

## Evaluation

This prevents fragmented ingress without making the routing skill responsible for conducting the review. Explicit early dispatch remains available, and ordinary internal workflow communication stays outside this interface.
