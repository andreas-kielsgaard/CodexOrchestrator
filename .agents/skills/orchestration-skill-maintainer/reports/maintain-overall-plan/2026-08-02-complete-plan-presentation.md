# Complete Overall Plan presentation

## Observation and theory

The observed Overall Plan task presented an oversimplified plan because the definition described plan contents without requiring a complete initial presentation. A first correction required the full plan on every maintenance pass, which unnecessarily repeated unaffected detail.

## Revision

`maintain-overall-plan` keeps the complete plan through every revision and presents it in full only when first established, when the user requests it, and after context compaction. Other maintenance passes may present the change and its consequences.

A full presentation includes the objective and completion boundary, evidence and current position, concern landscape, complete slice forecast, cross-slice relationships, risks and decisions, actual history, active ownership, and next judgment. Every forecast slice carries its movement, placement rationale, dependencies, concerns, and exit question.

## Evaluation

This preserves plan legibility at creation, on request, and after compaction without spending output on unchanged material during ordinary maintenance. The full plan remains high-level because Plan Step details stay inside Plan Slice conversations. Later slices may remain provisional and lower-resolution, avoiding false precision while keeping the route visible.

An initial post-compaction forward test returned only the objective, three slice statuses, and the next action. The role skill now requires reconstruction and presentation of every full-plan coverage area before another operation and states that a status recap is insufficient.

The corrected post-compaction test presented all eight Overall Plan areas and described every accepted, active, and forecast slice with its movement, placement, dependencies, concerns, exit condition, and evidenced status.
