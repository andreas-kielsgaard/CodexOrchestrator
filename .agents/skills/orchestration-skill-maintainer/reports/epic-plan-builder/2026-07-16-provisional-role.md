# Provisional Epic Plan Builder

Status: created. The existing Conversation Harness still references `orchestration-plan-builder`; no wiring changed.

## Trigger

The proposed flow begins with a working Plan Builder that submits structured state and requests initiation through session-bound semantic actions.

## Why adjacent skills are insufficient

`orchestration-plan-builder` also plans orchestration homes, record roots, and later ad-hoc startup. The product role needs only pre-initiation discussion, proposal submission, and authorized initiation request.

## Wording and expected effect

The skill separates discussion, submission, request, and observed transition. It prevents manual downstream handoff and identity arguments that the application can derive.
