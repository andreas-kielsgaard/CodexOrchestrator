# Plan Slice profile outcome calibration

## Observation

Later Plan Slices inherited Sol/extra-high, while earlier Sol/high Slices also completed and were accepted. The available evidence did not link extra-high reasoning to improved quality, and several slow or corrected Slices were better explained by task scope, environment, validation seams, long builds, or interruption.

## Reader and cause

The Overall Plan evaluates Slice completion and selects later Slice profiles. Earlier evaluation retained direction-changing evidence but no compact causal judgment about the profile, leaving defaults to change from impression rather than repeated outcomes.

## Revision

Record whether the Slice completed, whether its host-evidenced profile plausibly affected the result, and the stronger alternative cause when applicable. Include successful outcomes and adjust later selections only after repeated same-direction evidence.

## Evaluation

This supports adaptive selection without turning every correction or long run into a model escalation. The judgment stays at Initiative level and omits routine implementation detail.
