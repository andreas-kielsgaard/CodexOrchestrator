# Combined Validation Economy

## Observation and theory

Slice convergence needs integration evidence, but repeating every accepted Plan Step command does not establish more when the checkpoints are unchanged.

## Revision concept

Reuse exact Step evidence and select combined checks for risks created by composition, integration, or a previously uncovered Slice boundary. Preserve explicitly required independent and full-suite gates.

## Evaluation

This focuses convergence cost on Slice-level uncertainty without allowing expensive validation to defer acceptance criteria or weaken required gates.
