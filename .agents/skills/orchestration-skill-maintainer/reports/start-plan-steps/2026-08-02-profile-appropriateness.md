# Plan Step profile appropriateness

## Observation and theory

The Plan Slice role defined Luna/Terra and reasoning-selection axes, but `start-plan-steps` required only a planned model and reasoning value. It could launch a uniform Terra/high packet without evidence that each profile matched its own ambiguity and context scope.

## Revision

Every ready Plan Step now carries a visible profile assessment: ambiguity, context scope and blast radius, chosen model and reasoning, and why that choice fits better than adjacent settings. `start-plan-steps` treats a uniform or maximum profile without step-specific reasoning as not launch-ready.

## Evaluation

This still permits several genuinely broad or ambiguous steps to converge on Terra/high, but requires that conclusion to arise independently for each. It also preserves valid mixed profiles such as Luna/high or Terra/low and prevents stronger settings from substituting for decomposition or bounded context.

A fresh launch-readiness test supplied three ready steps all marked Terra/high without their ambiguity, context, blast-radius, or comparative reasoning. The reader launched none and returned the packet for planning revision, while preserving the possibility that all three profiles could be justified independently.
