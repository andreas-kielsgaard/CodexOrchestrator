# Review step readiness

## Observation and theory

Once a standing review was written into a Slice Plan, `start-plan-steps` treated it like any other ready item. The launch boundary had no check that the review contributed distinct required evidence rather than duplicating the Slice owner's evaluation.

## Revision

Before launching a review or verification step, the operation now confirms that its specification identifies independently required evidence or a distinct unresolved concern beyond ordinary Slice evaluation.

## Evaluation

This is a narrow launch guard. It does not prevent review work that has a concrete evidence purpose, and it avoids creating a separate task merely to repeat acceptance work already owned by the Slice conversation.

In fresh forward tests, the launch operation had no review task for high-risk work whose ordinary evidence was ready for Slice evaluation, while an explicitly required adversarial assessment remained launchable with its distinct threat-case-matrix deliverable.
