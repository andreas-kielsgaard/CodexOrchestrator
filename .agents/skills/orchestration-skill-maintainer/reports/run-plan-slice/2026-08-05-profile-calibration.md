# Plan Step profile calibration

## Observation

Recent Plan Steps generally used Luna for determinate work and Terra for ambiguous work, but terse selection language did not explain how ambiguity differs from context burden. Some broad cross-stack work plausibly received too little reasoning, while high settings were sometimes chosen uniformly even when delay or failure came from environment, test seams, build cost, or interruption.

## Reader and cause

The Plan Slice conversation selects every Plan Step profile before launch. Naming the two axes without operational clues left room to treat difficulty, importance, duration, and confidence as escalation signals. It also did not explain common mixed profiles such as Luna/high or Terra/low, or why Sol usually indicates that the outcome belongs at Slice level.

## Revision

Reformulated model choice around solution ambiguity and reasoning choice around context burden. Added broad, non-exhaustive clues for Luna, Terra, and low through high reasoning; separated profile selection from missing authority or validation capability; and made adjacent lower and higher comparisons part of every visible assessment.

## Evaluation

This should produce task-specific calibration without a rigid matrix. It keeps Luna and Terra flexible, discourages maximum-by-default selection, and prompts decomposition when task size rather than irreducible reasoning need drives escalation.
