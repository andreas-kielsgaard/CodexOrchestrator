# Plan Slice conversation independence

## Observation and theory

The new Slice handoff named earlier Slices and described what each had implemented. This encouraged historical ownership and transcript context to cross the Slice boundary even though the new conversation should independently plan from the accepted current state.

## Revision

The Slice grounds planning in its self-contained handoff, current repository or worktree, and authoritative artifacts. It discovers the resolution independently and treats conversation ids only as routing addresses. A missing material fact becomes an evidence gap, blocker, or reserved decision rather than a reason to read another conversation.

## Evaluation

The revision preserves direct callback routing and accepted repository continuity while preventing another conversation's planning or narrative from becoming evidence for this Slice.

A fresh read-only test supplied a complete handoff plus two earlier conversation ids described as optional implementation discussion. The Slice used only its handoff and authoritative artifact routes, declined to read either conversation, and treated the ids solely as routing data.
