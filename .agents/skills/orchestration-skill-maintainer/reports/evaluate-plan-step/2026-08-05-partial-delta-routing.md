# Partial actionability and delta routing

## Observation

Non-actionable partial callbacks caused repeated full evaluation turns and continuation prompts that restated accepted history. In sampled SRH-01 traffic, seven continuations repeated retained proof across roughly 13.5 thousand characters even though the next action remained with the same Plan Step. CLH-01 later returned a genuine initiation decision together with an immature correction candidate; the Slice launched independent audit before local provenance and tool-safety evidence were coherent.

## Reader and cause

The reader decides what a returned Step makes actionable for its Slice. Existing wording tested whether any Slice action existed, but not whether that action was bundled with unfinished work that still belonged to the Step.

## Revision

Test partial actionability and candidate maturity separately. Resolve a genuine Slice action while returning the same Step to finish its local boundary; independent evaluation waits for the coherent candidate already required by the Slice Plan. Continuation and correction packets carry only the task-local delta and rely on retained task state for unchanged material.

## Evaluation

This should reduce repeated evidence ingestion and premature audits while preserving genuine parent decisions and same-owner correction. It does not require the Slice to trust an unverified checkpoint.
