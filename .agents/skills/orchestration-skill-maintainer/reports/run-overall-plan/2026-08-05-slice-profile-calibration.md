# Plan Slice profile calibration

## Observation

Eight later Plan Slice conversations inherited Sol with extra-high reasoning because creation omitted explicit fields. Earlier Sol/high Slices also completed and were independently accepted, and the investigation found no quality evidence supporting a uniform extra-high uplift.

## Reader and cause

The Overall Plan conversation selects profiles for ready Plan Slices. The former one-line Sol/high default did not explain why the role normally needs that profile, when Terra or medium reasoning could fit, or what exceptional evidence would justify extra-high. Importance and breadth could therefore be mistaken for automatic escalation.

## Revision

Keep Sol/high as a role-based starting point while assessing model from actual synthesis demand and reasoning from context burden. Explain bounded departures, treat size as a decomposition clue, exclude duration and importance as escalation reasons, and require comparison with adjacent settings. Use later outcomes for causal calibration rather than assuming model choice caused every correction.

## Evaluation

The additional clues should make departures deliberate without making Sol/high mandatory. They preserve room for unusual Slices while reducing inherited maximum settings and preventing narrow fixed outcomes from being disguised as highly reasoned Slice planning.
