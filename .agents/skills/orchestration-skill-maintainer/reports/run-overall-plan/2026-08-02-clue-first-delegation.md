# Clue-first Plan Slice delegation

## Observation and theory

The handoff to Plan Slice `019fc21d-1e60-7762-94be-6d4c46543329` exported much of the Overall Plan's reasoning as a long set of child instructions. Even after separating role mechanics from task content, the role lacked a principle distinguishing useful task clues from parent-authored procedure.

## Revision

The Overall Plan keeps detailed cross-slice reasoning in its own conversation. A child receives its movement, present evidence, genuine boundaries, acceptance, and a few broad clues. Prescriptive instructions are reserved for hard task boundaries such as authority, safety, scope, dependency, or acceptance.

## Evaluation

This preserves clear ownership and hard constraints while leaving the Plan Slice to discover its detailed plan under `run-plan-slice`. The risk of under-specification is limited by retaining exact objective, evidence, authority, boundaries, and completion conditions.

An initial forward test still mentioned the parent's unaccepted architecture hypothesis and converted rejecting that hypothesis into a child rule. The wording was tightened so clues name neutral concerns, evidence, or surfaces to inspect while unaccepted solution candidates and command or check suggestions remain in the parent plan.

A fresh test silently omitted the proposed table, MCP tool, controllers, and exact commands. It passed only the durable-activation objective, hard boundaries, acceptance, and neutral clues about persistence, retries, concurrency, restart, failure, and truthful observation.
