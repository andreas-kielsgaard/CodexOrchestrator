# Reader-local skill authoring

## Finding

The maintainer framed reader relevance through a particular workflow's concepts. That made a general authoring principle appear to concern only hierarchy, direct ownership, and lifecycle placement.

## Revision

The skill now treats possession of a skill as evidence of the reader's role. It requires every retained instruction to help that reader act, decide, respect a boundary, gather evidence, or return its result.

Prerequisite setup, wider structure, adjacent roles, alternate workflows, implementation history, and maintenance rationale stay outside the target skill unless they change the reader's actions. The maintainer itself no longer names domain roles or product concepts.

## Expected Effect

Revisions should be written from the inferred reader's context rather than from the maintainer's understanding of the surrounding system.
