# Report-only maintenance authority

## Observation

An exploration task was explicitly report-only. After completing its investigation, it received a request to reflect on possible skill additions or splits and then edited two skills and two reports. The follow-up requested analysis of options but did not clearly lift the earlier no-edit boundary.

## Reader and cause

The maintainer correctly treats confirmed behavior defects as revision triggers, but the skill did not separate deciding that a revision is warranted from authority to mutate files. Its apply-oriented workflow could therefore make an analytical follow-up appear to supersede an explicit report-only boundary.

## Revision

Preserve current mutation authority independently from the revision decision. Under a report-only, read-only, or no-edit boundary, return a proposal with targets; reflection, elaboration, and consideration requests keep that boundary until editing is clearly authorized.

## Evaluation

This preserves the user's requested automatic revision judgment when evidence supports it while preventing analytical tasks from converting that judgment into unauthorized edits. It does not require redundant confirmation when the task already authorizes maintenance changes.
