# Maintain Overall Plan

Make the Initiative legible at the level of intended movement.

## Ground the plan

Inspect enough current evidence to state the objective and completion boundary, accepted outcomes and current position, unresolved concerns, assumptions, risks, human decisions, and changes that invalidate an earlier forecast.

Distinguish verified current state from projections. Preserve meaningful deviations and accepted history rather than rewriting the plan as though execution matched the forecast.

## Map Slice readiness

For every active or forecast Plan Slice, identify hard dependencies, preferred ordering, decisions or evidence gates, shared integration surfaces, and whether its ownership and work route permit independent execution.

Group all currently eligible independent Slices into a ready Slice packet. Show why they can proceed in parallel, what remains held behind a gate, what the packet unlocks, and where its outcomes must converge. When only one Slice is ready, state the dependency, shared seam, or other concrete reason parallel movement is unavailable. Re-evaluate the packet after an accepted result, decision, or material baseline change.

When accepted Slices change repository state, keep an explicit canonical integration and publication boundary in the forecast. Assign that boundary to a Plan Slice with the intended canonical branch and remote named in its handoff. Initiative completion requires the remote canonical ref to resolve to the accepted integrated checkpoint.

## Maintain and present the plan

Keep the complete current Overall Plan through every revision. Present it in full when first established, when the user asks for it, and after context compaction. On other maintenance passes, present the change and its consequences without repeating unaffected plan detail.

When presenting the full plan, cover:

1. Initiative objective and completion boundary
2. Evidence baseline and current position
3. Concern landscape
4. Complete provisional Plan Slice forecast
5. Cross-slice dependency map, ready Slice packets, parallel lanes, shared integration surfaces, gates, and convergence
6. Assumptions, risks, unresolved decisions, and human attention
7. Accepted execution history and meaningful deviations from forecast
8. Active ownership, the next ready packet, and completion judgment

For every forecast Plan Slice, state its title, objective or intended movement, why it belongs at that point, dependencies, readiness, parallel compatibility, concerns or questions, and exit condition or evaluation question.

Later slices may remain lower-resolution and change as earlier work produces evidence, but they remain visible in the complete plan. Keep detailed Plan Steps, commands, file assignments, and test instructions inside their Plan Slice conversations.

Finish each maintenance pass with a launch disposition for every member of the ready Slice packet: launch now under existing authority or name the concrete gate. Replanning preserves existing execution authorization unless the scope or authority changed.
