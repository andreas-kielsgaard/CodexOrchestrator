# Maintain Overall Plan

Make the Initiative legible at the level of intended movement.

## Ground the plan

Inspect enough current evidence to state:

- the objective and completion boundary;
- accepted outcomes and the current position;
- unresolved concerns, assumptions, risks, and human decisions; and
- material changes that invalidate an earlier forecast.

Distinguish verified current state from projections. Preserve meaningful deviations and accepted history rather than rewriting the plan as though execution matched the forecast.

## Structure the plan

Present a compact concern map and a provisional sequence of Plan Slices. Show dependencies, likely parallel movement, and evaluation points where useful.

For each forecast slice, state:

- its title and intended movement;
- why it belongs at that point;
- the concerns or questions it is expected to address; and
- the exit condition or evaluation question.

Later slices remain low-resolution and may change as earlier work produces evidence. Do not turn the Overall Plan into detailed Plan Steps, commands, file assignments, or test instructions.

## State the next movement

Name the next genuine Plan Slice, a required human decision, or Initiative completion.
