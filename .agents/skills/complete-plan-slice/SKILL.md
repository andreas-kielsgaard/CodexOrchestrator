# Complete Plan Slice

Evaluate the slice as a combined result, not merely as a collection of accepted Plan Steps.

Check the slice objective and exit condition, integration and coherence across results, required validation, regressions or standards relevant to the slice, residual risks, deferrals, and unproven boundaries.

If required work remains, identify it and leave the slice open. When the slice is complete or requires an Overall Plan decision, prepare one compact report containing:

- the result and accepted movement;
- the evidence supporting acceptance;
- repository or checkpoint state where relevant;
- remaining gaps, explicit deferrals, and unproven claims; and
- implications for later slices or the Overall Plan.
